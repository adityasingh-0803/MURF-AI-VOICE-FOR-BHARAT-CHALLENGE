# Day 6 — Outbound Calls

## Track

Learning & Literacy

## Use Case

Daily practice calls.

The learner opts into a daily practice schedule and
ShikshaSaathi calls at the selected time.

## Opening

The agent says:

"Namaste, this is ShikshaSaathi, your learning assistant.
I'm calling for your daily practice session that you
previously opted into. Is this a good time?

You can say stop at any time and I won't make further
practice calls."

The opening communicates:

- Who is calling
- Why the call is happening
- How to stop future calls

## Telephony

Twilio Programmable Voice is used for outbound calls.

## Voice

Murf is used as the TTS layer.

Keep the Murf API key server-side.

## Demo

Use a phone number you control.

Do not make unsolicited bulk calls.
